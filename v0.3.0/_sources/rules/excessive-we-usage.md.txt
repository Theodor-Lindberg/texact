# excessive-we-usage (UNS002)

## What it does

Counts occurrences of `we` and reports the rule when the configured maximum is
exceeded. This by default five instances: one in the abstract, one in the introduction,
one in the conclusions, and then two extra for good measure.

## Why is this bad?

Excessive use of the word is not suitable for academic writing.

## Example

```latex
We show that we can improve the design, and we evaluate what we propose.
```
